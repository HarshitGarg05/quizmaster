# QuizMaster Design System: "The Cognitive Atelier"

The visual identity of QuizMaster has transitioned from a high-energy neon aesthetic to a refined, editorial "Cognitive Atelier" style. This design emphasizes clarity, intellectual focus, and a premium academic atmosphere.

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Principles
- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist Editorial. Prioritizes negative space, high-contrast typography, and soft depth.
- **Surface Strategy**: Light mode by default. Uses "Glass Panels" with 20px blur and subtle shadows (`rgba(11, 15, 16, 0.06)`).
- **Interactivity**: Scale-down (`0.95x`) on click, subtle hover opacity shifts, and smooth transitions. No bulky borders.

## 🌈 Color Palette (Tailwind Tokens)
- **Primary**: `#0050d4` (Lumina Deep Blue)
- **Primary Container**: `#7b9cff` (Soft Azure)
- **Surface**: `#f5f7f9` (Cool Grey White)
- **On-Surface**: `#2c2f31` (Deep Obsidian Ink)
- **On-Surface Variant**: `#595c5e` (Muted Slate)
- **Secondary Container**: `#d5e3fc` (Pale Blue accent)
- **Error**: `#b31b25` (Rose Red)
- **Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #0050d4 0%, #7b9cff 100%)` (Editorial Gradient)

## ✍️ Typography
- **Headings (Headline)**: `Manrope` (ExtraBold tracking-tight). Used for impact and authority.
- **Body / Labels**: `Inter` (Regular/Medium). Clean, modern, and highly legible.

## 📐 Components & Icons
- **Icons**: Material Symbols Outlined (Opsz: 24, Wght: 400).
- **Corners**: `lg` (0.5rem), `xl` (0.75rem), `3xl` (1.5rem for large containers).
- **Navigation**: 20px height, fixed with 80% blur background (`bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-xl`).

## 🌌 Creative Elements
- **Parallax Backgrounds**: Use minimalist artistic illustrations or geometric shapes with low opacity (10-15%) that shift slowly during scroll.
- **Glassmorphism**: Applied to dashboards and cards for a "holographic paper" feel.
